Settings

CleanTabs saves tabs as notes for a CleanNotes account that syncs with S3: in a tabs/ directory of its folder, which CleanNotes shows as its own CleanTabs section, apart from your notes. Use the same settings as that account in CleanNotes (Settings → Accounts).

Pictures of pages

CleanTabs takes the picture when a page has loaded or is shown, so it needs access to the pages you visit (Chrome asks). Pictures are about 15 KB each, kept with the notes in your bucket; tabs saved before being shown get none.
